To stop receiving updates, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of a Buttondown email from this blog, or [manage your subscription in the reader portal](https://buttondown.com/portal). The portal will email you a sign-in link.

If you subscribed before the move to Buttondown, reply to one of the earlier newsletters and ask me to remove you from the mailing list.
